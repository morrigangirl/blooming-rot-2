# Blooming Rot 2 — Fix Ledger

The running list. Every finding from every adversarial review lives here until it's closed.

## How this file works

- Each item has a **stable ID** (`P2-07`, `P3-14`, `TRAVEL-02`). IDs are never reused or renumbered, even when an item is closed.
- **Open items** are listed plainly.
- **Closed items** are ~~struck through~~ and carry a short note saying what was done and which release it shipped in.
- **Overturned items** — findings that turned out to be wrong — are struck through and marked *overturned*, with what's actually true. They stay on the list so nobody re-files them.
- **Parked items** are real but waiting on something (an asset, a decision, a later phase pass). They keep their ID and stay visible.
- New adversarial passes **append** to the relevant phase section. They do not renumber anything.
